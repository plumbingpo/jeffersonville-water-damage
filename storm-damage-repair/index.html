Signs You Need Storm Damage Repair in Jeffersonville

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Water in the lowest level after storm drains back up or overflow
  • Swollen ceiling panels or blistered, peeling paint in the days after a storm
  • Missing, damaged or lifted shingles you can see from the ground
  • Water stains that show up on ceilings or walls during or after a storm
  • Rooms that start to smell stale or moldy following a storm
  • Water pooling in the attic, around the chimney or near skylights
  • Gutters or downspouts torn loose, bent or dumping water against the house

How to Prevent Storm Damage Repair Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Take dated photos of the house every year, since insurers ask about prior condition
  • Clogged gutters overflow in a downpour, so clear them and the downspouts often
  • Extend downspouts and slope the soil so runoff moves away from the foundation
  • Cut back limbs that hang over or close to the house so wind cannot throw them into it
  • Ice dams form when attics run warm, so check that yours has enough insulation and airflow
  • Check window and door seals each year and replace any caulking that has cracked
